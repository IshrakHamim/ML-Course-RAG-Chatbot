# Northbridge Institute — Student Services Guide (Fall 2026)

## Library

The Kessler Library is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 22:00, and on weekends from
10:00 to 18:00. During exam weeks (weeks 8 and 15) it stays open until midnight every day.
Group study rooms can be booked for up to three hours per day through the library portal.

## Orca GPU cluster access

Students get an Orca account automatically on the first day of the semester. Log in with
your Northbridge username and the one-time code sent to your student email. Support tickets
go to orca-help@northbridge.example and are answered within one working day. Scheduled
maintenance happens every first Sunday of the month from 06:00 to 12:00.

## Accessibility and exam accommodations

Students who need extra exam time, a quiet room, or assistive software must register with
the Accessibility Office before the end of week 4. Approved accommodations are sent directly
to course staff; students do not need to share medical details with lecturers.

## Rescheduling an exam

A missed exam can only be rescheduled for documented illness, a family emergency, or an
official sports or academic event. Submit the request form within five working days of the
exam. Rescheduled exams take place during the resit week in January.

## Student wellbeing

Free counselling sessions are available through the Wellbeing Centre in the Mendel
Building. Drop-in hours are Wednesdays from 13:00 to 15:00; other appointments can be booked
online.
